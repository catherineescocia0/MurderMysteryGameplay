Case Overview

On Friday, November 15th, 2024, at 11:47 PM, security discovered the body of Victor Sterling, 45-year-old CEO of Sterling Tech, in his private office on the 12th floor. The victim was found lying face-down near his desk with severe head trauma. Time of death is estimated between 10:30 PM and 11:15 PM.

The building's security logs show that four individuals were still in the building at the time of death: Marcus Webb (CFO), Diana Cole (Head of R&D), Robert Hayes (Lead Developer), and Jennifer Park (Executive Assistant). All four had access to the executive floor.

Your mission: Review all evidence, witness statements, and clues to determine who killed Victor Sterling and how they committed the crime.

Summary of the Crime

Marcus Webb murdered Victor Sterling to prevent the exposure of his $2.3 million embezzlement scheme and avoid prosecution. Webb used a calculated plan involving drugging the victim and staging the scene to cast suspicion on others, but critical forensic evidence and timeline analysis revealed his guilt.

Key Evidence Against Marcus Webb

Fingerprints on Coffee Cup Marcus's fingerprints were the PRIMARY prints on the coffee cup, not Diana's. While Diana brought the coffee, Marcus handled it when drugging it. Diana's secondary prints are from when she prepared and carried the cup.
Security Access Logs Marcus accessed the 12th floor at 10:43 PM, exactly 3 minutes after Victor sent the text "Come to my office NOW." This text was sent after Victor discovered the embezzlement email draft at 10:42 PM.
The Murder Weapon The glass award that killed Victor was Marcus's own "CFO of the Year 2023" award that had been displayed on Victor's desk. Marcus used his own award in a moment of rage - a deeply symbolic choice.
Financial Motive Marcus had embezzled $2.3 million and was about to be exposed. Victor's laptop contained an unsent email to the board detailing the embezzlement, drafted just 9 minutes before Marcus arrived.
Flight Plans Marcus purchased a one-way ticket to the Cayman Islands departing the morning after the murder. This was purchased two days before the murder, suggesting premeditation of his escape.
The Lie in His Statement Marcus claimed Victor's office was dark when he arrived at 10:45 PM, but security footage and forensic evidence prove Victor was alive and the lights were on until at least 10:51 PM.

Why the Other Suspects Are Innocent

Diana Cole: Left the building before the murder. Her fingerprints on the cup are from preparing and delivering the coffee. Security logs show no return to 12th floor.

Robert Hayes: Computer logs prove continuous coding activity from 8 PM to 11:30 PM on 10th floor. Never accessed 12th floor. Alibi is airtight and verified by digital timestamps.

Jennifer Park: Security confirms she left the building at 10:05 PM and never returned. Building entry/exit logs are biometric and cannot be faked.
