Incident Overview

Initial Discovery Report

On January 8, 2025, at 11:34 PM, Dr. Nathan Pierce, 41-year-old quantum physicist and lead researcher at the Meridian Institute for Advanced Physics, was found dead in Laboratory 7 of the institute's underground research facility. The cause of death has been determined as cardiac arrest induced by a massive electrical shock delivered through a modified Tesla coil apparatus.

The circumstances surrounding Dr. Pierce's death defy conventional investigation methods. Security footage from Laboratory 7 shows Dr. Pierce working alone from 9:00 PM until his death at 11:34 PM. However, the footage contains an unexplainable anomaly: at exactly 11:33:47 PM (thirteen seconds before his death), the camera captures what appears to be TWO instances of Dr. Pierce simultaneously present in the laboratory - one at his workstation and another standing near the Tesla coil apparatus. Both figures are solid, three-dimensional, and performing independent actions.

This temporal duplication persists for exactly 13 seconds before both figures converge at the Tesla coil, at which point one Dr. Pierce activates the device while the other reaches toward it. The resulting electrical discharge kills both instances simultaneously. The footage shows both figures collapsing, but when security arrived at 11:36 PM, only ONE body was present.

Quantum Anomaly Profile

Primary Anomaly: Temporal Duplication Event - Subject existed in two locations simultaneously for 13 seconds

Death Mechanism: Electrical shock from Tesla coil (240,000 volts, 3 seconds duration)

Paradox Status: UNRESOLVED - Cause precedes effect in violation of causality

Witnesses: None - All personnel were evacuated from facility at 10:00 PM for "routine quantum containment protocol"

Time of Death: 11:34:00 PM - Confirmed by security footage, facility sensors, and medical examination

Dr. Pierce was the lead researcher on Project Everett, a classified quantum computing initiative exploring practical applications of the Many-Worlds Interpretation of quantum mechanics. His work involved creating localized quantum superposition states in macroscopic objects - a theoretical framework that might explain his temporal duplication.

Three days before his death, Dr. Pierce sent an encrypted email to the Meridian Institute's Board of Directors claiming he had achieved "consciousness-level quantum entanglement" and warning that someone was "sabotaging the experiment to prevent disclosure." The email was timestamped January 5, 2025, 2:47 AM, but facility logs show Dr. Pierce was not on the premises at that time. His access card shows he entered the facility at 8:00 AM on January 5th - six hours AFTER the email was sent.

Lead Investigator's Assessment: This case represents an unprecedented intersection of murder investigation and quantum mechanics. The evidence suggests Dr. Pierce was killed by electrical shock, but the mechanism of his death involves a temporal causality loop that defies classical physics. Key questions: (1) How did two instances of Dr. Pierce exist simultaneously? (2) Which instance was the "original" and which was the duplicate? (3) Did Dr. Pierce kill himself, or was the duplicate created by an external party to murder him? (4) If someone else created the duplicate, they would need intimate knowledge of Project Everett and access to equipment capable of generating quantum superposition in a human consciousness. Every person who fits this profile is a potential suspect - but the true killer may not even be aware they committed murder, as the quantum mechanics involved suggest the act of observation itself may have collapsed a superposition state, inadvertently creating the murderous duplicate. This investigation requires us to question the very nature of identity, causality, and whether someone can be guilty of murder when the weapon is a version of the victim himself.
