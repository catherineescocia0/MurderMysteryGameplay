Case Overview

Initial Discovery Report

On Saturday, November 16, 2024, at approximately 3:47 PM, Tyler Morrison (age 15) and Marcus Chen (age 15) discovered a severely mutilated body in the basement of an abandoned residential property located at 847 Elm Street in Oakridge. The discovery occurred when Tyler entered the property after Marcus dared him to explore the allegedly "haunted" house.

The victim, an unidentified male, was found in an advanced state of decomposition with the face completely severed from the skull and four fingers surgically amputated: three from the left hand (pinky, ring finger, and pointer finger) and one from the right hand (thumb). The amputations were performed with medical precision, showing clean cuts through the metacarpal bones.

The deliberate destruction of identifying features - facial removal and strategic finger amputation - was clearly intended to prevent identification through standard methods. However, the perpetrator left behind clues that suggest this was not random violence but a carefully orchestrated murder with a message.

Victim Profile (Preliminary)

Identity Status: UNKNOWN - Initially catalogued as "John Doe #47"

Later Identified As: Jacob "Jake" Morrison, age 32

Physical Description: Male, Caucasian, 5'11" (180cm), athletic build

Distinguishing Features: Surgical scar on right knee (ACL repair approximately 3 years old), partial tattoo on upper left arm (text reads "Semper Fi" - Marine Corps motto)

Time of Death: Wednesday, November 13, 2024, between 10:00 PM - 2:00 AM

Cause of Death: Exsanguination from severed femoral artery in left thigh

Confirmed Identity Method: DNA match to Jake Morrison through military service records; tattoo confirmed Marine Corps service 2012-2016

The abandoned house at 847 Elm Street has been vacant for eight months following foreclosure proceedings. The property is currently owned by Oakridge Community Bank. No utilities are active at the location. Despite the home's abandonment, evidence indicates recent habitation in the basement area.

Found at the scene: a sleeping bag, portable camping lantern, empty food containers (forensically dated within two weeks of discovery), and critically - scattered papers around the body spelling out "JAKE" when properly arranged. Additionally, a prescription bottle labeled "J. Morrison" was discovered in a black duffel bag found approximately 10 feet from the body.

Lead Investigator's Critical Finding: The case initially appeared to be a stranger murder with identity concealment. However, forensic analysis revealed that the four missing fingers were NOT taken as trophies by the killer - they were left at specific locations as deliberate clues. Three fingers (left pinky, left ring, left pointer) were discovered on November 18th by transit authorities inside Bus #447, which runs the Oakridge-Portland route. The fourth finger (right thumb) was located during autopsy - it had been placed INSIDE the victim's stomach, requiring him to have swallowed it before death. This discovery fundamentally changes the nature of the investigation. The victim knowingly swallowed his own severed finger. The other three fingers were transported via bus to a specific destination. This suggests the victim was complicit in his own mutilation, potentially under extreme duress, or the killing involved a level of psychological torture that forced participation. The scattered papers spelling "JAKE" were not random - they came from a torn prescription authorization document. Every element of this crime scene was staged with purpose. The question is not just who killed Jake Morrison, but why he participated in the destruction of his own identity.

Left hand showing amputation of three fingers: pinky (5th digit), ring finger (4th digit), and pointer finger (2nd digit). Middle finger and thumb remain intact. Minimal bleeding suggests amputations occurred near or after time of death.
EXHIBIT 4: HAND MUTILATION - RIGHT
Right hand showing amputation of thumb (1st digit). Four fingers remain intact. Identical cutting precision as left hand. Medical examiner notes: The specific pattern of finger removal is NOT random. Three fingers recovered from Bus #447 (Oakridge-Portland route) on November 18, 2024. Found wrapped in plastic bag wedged behind seat 23B. DNA confirms match to victim. Right thumb discovered during autopsy in victim's stomach. Victim swallowed his own severed thumb. Forensic Analysis

FORENSIC REPORT #001
Cause and Manner of Death
Cause of death: Exsanguination from severed left femoral artery. The wound is a single deep incision, 4.2 inches in length, penetrating 2.3 inches deep, severing the femoral artery completely. Blood loss analysis indicates death occurred within 4-6 minutes of the injury. The angle of the cut (78 degrees relative to the horizontal plane of the body) and the depth progression (deep to shallow, medial to lateral) are biomechanically consistent with a self-inflicted wound. However, the victim's right hand is missing its thumb - the dominant gripping digit. Grip strength testing on a blade simulator shows it would be nearly impossible to achieve the necessary pressure and control for this wound without a functional thumb on the dominant hand. CONCLUSION: Wound appears self-inflicted but circumstances suggest coercion or assistance. Alternative theory: The wound was inflicted by a second party, but the victim was positioned and held in such a way to mimic self-infliction. Manner of death: HOMICIDE (uncertain if victim was forced to participate in own death).
FORENSIC REPORT #002
Timeline of Mutilations
Forensic pathology establishes the following sequence: (1) Chemical burns to six fingers occurred 6-12 hours before death (estimated November 13, 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM), (2) Right thumb amputation occurred while victim was alive, approximately 15-30 minutes before death, (3) Three left-hand fingers amputated within 10 minutes of death (victim possibly still conscious), (4) Femoral artery severed resulting in death, (5) Facial removal occurred 2-4 hours POST-mortem. The victim experienced his mutilation in stages over multiple hours. The chemical burns suggest premeditation - someone prepared by destroying fingerprints hours before the killing. The thumb amputation and subsequent forced swallowing indicates extreme psychological torture. The victim knew he was going to die and was made to participate in the destruction of his own identity. The three fingers removed near death were placed on Bus #447 by an unknown party between 11:00 PM - 11:30 PM on November 13.
FORENSIC REPORT #003
Chemical Analysis - Fingerprint Destruction
Six fingers on the victim's hands show evidence of chemical exposure consistent with concentrated hydrochloric acid (HCl) at approximately 30-35% concentration. The acid was applied topically to the finger pads, destroying the epidermal and dermal layers containing fingerprint ridges. Application method: soaking (fingers were submerged in acid for 8-12 minutes based on tissue damage depth). Time of exposure: 6-12 hours before death. The specific fingers destroyed were the left thumb, left middle finger, right index finger, right middle finger, right ring finger, and right pinky. The SPARED fingers (those later amputated) were the only ones with viable fingerprints remaining. This creates a paradox: Why destroy six fingerprints but preserve four, only to then remove those four fingers? Answer: The perpetrator needed the four viable fingerprints to EXIST somewhere other than on the body. The fingers were removed and transported elsewhere. This suggests the fingerprints themselves had value - possibly to plant evidence, create a false identity trail, or prove the victim's presence at a location.

Movement Analysis Conclusion: Jake Morrison's movements show a methodical investigation spanning four weeks. He tracked someone from Chicago to Oakridge, surveilled them, confronted associates (TC, Marcus Chen, David Hargrove), collected evidence, and arranged a final meeting at the location where he was ultimately killed. The meeting site - 847 Elm Street - is significant because it was David Hargrove's former home before foreclosure. Hargrove was the first responder to Robert Morrison's "accident" in 2016 when he worked as sheriff's deputy. The connections form a web: Jake investigates his father's death → trails David Hargrove → confronts Hargrove in Portland → returns to Hargrove's old house → dies in that same house. Either Hargrove killed Jake in revenge for the confrontation, OR someone else connected to the 2016 incident killed Jake to protect secrets he uncovered. The fingers on Bus #447 heading to Portland suggest the killer wanted something connected to Portland - possibly to frame Hargrove or send a message.
