Case Overview

Discovery Report

On Saturday, November 16, 2024, at approximately 3:47 PM, the severely decomposed body of an unidentified individual was discovered in the basement of an abandoned residential property at 847 Elm Street in the town of Oakridge. The discovery was made by Tyler Morrison, age 15, who entered the property on a dare from his friend Marcus Chen.

The body was found in an advanced state of decomposition, with the face completely severed from the skull - likely post-mortem based on minimal blood spatter patterns. More disturbingly, the victim was missing four fingers: three from the left hand (pinky, ring, and pointer) and one from the right hand (thumb). The fingers were removed with surgical precision, showing clean cuts at the metacarpal joints consistent with medical-grade cutting tools.

Due to the facial mutilation and fingerprint destruction (the removed fingers were the only ones with intact prints), standard identification methods have been rendered impossible. No dental records have matched the victim's remaining dental structure. The body has been deceased for an estimated 72-96 hours based on entomological evidence and decomposition rate analysis.

Estimated Age: 28-35 years (based on bone density and dental wear)

Height: 5'10" (178 cm)

Build: Athletic, muscular development consistent with regular physical training

Distinguishing Features: Surgical scar on right knee (ACL repair, approximately 2-3 years old), tattoo remnant on upper left arm (mostly decomposed but appears to be partial text)

Time of Death: Estimated Wednesday, November 13, 2024, between 8:00 PM - 2:00 AM

Cause of Death: Exsanguination from severed femoral artery in left thigh - appears to be intentional cut, not defensive wound

The abandoned house at 847 Elm Street has been vacant for 8 months following foreclosure. The property is owned by Oakridge Community Bank. No utilities are active. The basement shows signs of recent activity despite the home's abandonment - a sleeping bag, portable camping lantern, empty food containers (all forensically dated to within 2 weeks of body discovery), and disturbing evidence of the crime itself.

Lead Investigator's Analysis: This case presents unprecedented complexity. The perpetrator went to extraordinary lengths to prevent victim identification - facial removal, strategic finger amputation targeting only digits with intact fingerprints. However, they left behind a curious puzzle: scattered papers around the body spell out the name "JAKE" when arranged properly. This appears intentional - but is it the victim's name, the killer's name, or a deliberate misdirection? The prescription bottle found with the body lists a "J. Morrison" but pharmacy records show no such prescription issued in the past 5 years. The crime scene suggests both meticulous planning and potential staging. Multiple suspects have emerged, each with concerning connections to the victim or location. Solving this requires exceptional forensic analysis and logical deduction.

Forensic Evidence Analysis

FORENSIC REPORT #001
Finger Amputation Analysis
The four missing fingers were removed with surgical precision using a blade consistent with a #10 scalpel. Cut marks show no hesitation, suggesting experience with anatomical dissection. CRITICAL FINDING: The specific fingers removed (left pinky, ring, pointer; right thumb) are the ONLY fingers that retained fingerprint ridges. The other six fingers on the body show chemical burns consistent with hydrofluoric acid exposure - fingerprints destroyed but fingers left attached. This selective removal pattern indicates the perpetrator knew which fingers still had viable prints and surgically removed only those. This suggests either: (A) Perpetrator examined the body closely before mutilation, OR (B) Perpetrator has medical/forensic knowledge and knew standard fingerprint destruction methods and their efficacy.
FORENSIC REPORT #002
Cause of Death - Femoral Artery Severance
Victim died from a single deep incision to the left femoral artery, located in the upper inner thigh. Blood loss analysis indicates death occurred within 3-5 minutes of the cut. ANOMALY: The angle of the cut (82 degrees relative to horizontal body plane) and the depth progression (shallow to deep, left to right) are biomechanically consistent with a self-inflicted wound. However, three factors contradict suicide: (1) No cutting implement found at scene, (2) Victim's right hand (dominant based on muscle development) is missing thumb - making it nearly impossible to grip a blade firmly enough for this wound, (3) Tox screen shows 0.08% blood alcohol - present but not enough to explain suicidal ideation. Conclusion: Wound appears self-inflicted but circumstances suggest coercion or staging.
FORENSIC REPORT #003
Time of Death - Entomological Evidence
Blow fly larvae (Calliphoridae family) collected from body are in third instar stage. Temperature data from weather station shows average basement temp of 58°F (14.4°C). Using accumulated degree hours calculation: larvae development indicates eggs were laid 72-84 hours before discovery (3:47 PM Saturday). This places death between 8:00 PM Wednesday and 4:00 AM Thursday. Rigor mortis completely resolved. Livor mortis fixed in supine position matching found position - body was not moved post-mortem. Putrefaction stage consistent with 72-96 hour timeline. CONCLUSION: Death occurred Wednesday night, November 13th, most likely between 10:00 PM - 1:00 AM.
FORENSIC REPORT #004
The "JAKE" Papers - Document Analysis
Four pieces of paper found scattered around body. Each contains a single letter when properly oriented: J-A-K-E. Paper analysis: Standard 20lb copy paper, available at any office supply store. Ink analysis: Laser printer toner, HP brand, common model. However, CRITICAL DETAIL: Paper edges show deliberate tearing pattern - all four pieces came from a single sheet that was torn into quarters. Original document reconstruction shows the full page contained printed text: "JAKE MORRISON - PRESCRIPTION AUTHORIZATION - Patient ID: 47821". This was not random letters - it was a torn prescription authorization form. The name "Jake Morrison" appears to be authentic document text, not a staged clue. Question: Why would the killer leave this specific document, torn to highlight the name?
FORENSIC REPORT #005
Prescription Bottle Evidence
Empty prescription bottle found in victim's bag. Label reads: "J. Morrison - Oxycodone 10mg - Dr. Sarah Chen - Fill Date: March 2024". However, pharmacy database search reveals NO prescription for any "J. Morrison" filled at any Oakridge pharmacy in the past 3 years. Dr. Sarah Chen (local physician) states she has never written an oxycodone prescription for any patient named Morrison. Label analysis shows it is a genuine pharmacy label, not printed on standard equipment. DNA on bottle lip matches victim. CONCLUSION: Bottle is authentic but the prescription record has been erased from pharmacy databases, OR the victim obtained the bottle through illegal means and had a fake label applied, OR the prescription was issued under a false identity.

FORENSIC REPORT #010
USB Drive - Encrypted Files
USB drive found in bag contains 3 encrypted files: "evidence.pdf", "timeline.xlsx", "faces.jpg". Standard AES-256 encryption. Forensic analysis unable to crack encryption (would require estimated 10^68 years with current computing power). However, drive metadata reveals files were created/modified on November 8, 2024, at 2:17 PM. Device ID embedded in USB drive traces to a computer at Oakridge Public Library, Terminal #3. Library records show Terminal #3 was used between 2:00-3:00 PM on Nov 8 by someone who signed in as "J. Morrison". Security footage from library confirms an individual matching victim's build used the terminal. Librarian Theresa Cole (TC?) remembers the user asking about "data encryption software". CONCLUSION: Victim deliberately encrypted evidence he gathered. Password likely known only to victim. Without password, contents remain unknown.
FORENSIC REPORT #011

Sleeping bag and portable lantern found in basement. Sleeping bag shows evidence of use - body oils, skin cells, hair matching victim. Thermal imaging residue analysis indicates sleeping bag was used 2-4 nights before death (approximately Nov 9-11). Victim was living in the abandoned house. Lantern battery partially depleted, consistent with 8-12 hours of use. NO food containers, water bottles, or personal hygiene items found - suggesting victim left the house periodically for supplies. QUESTION: Why was victim living in an abandoned house? Why this specific house? Property records show house was foreclosed from the Hargrove family in March 2024. Hargroves moved to Portland (same city victim visited on Nov 10).
FORENSIC REPORT #012
Toxicology Full Panel

Trace amounts of caffeine consistent with coffee consumption 4-6 hours before death. UNEXPECTED FINDING: Elevated cortisol levels (stress hormone) - measurements 3x normal baseline. This indicates victim was under extreme psychological stress in final hours of life. Adrenaline levels also elevated. Heart rate at time of death estimated 110-120 BPM (normal resting is 60-80).
